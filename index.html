About me

Most operations problems are really information problems.

I spent nearly seven years at Survey Monster running the floor — as data manager, then as operations manager for Marquee Mall BPO Seats. The pattern repeated: teams underperforming were rarely working less hard than anyone else. They just couldn't see what was happening in time to act.

So I built the visibility first. KPI tracking that updated weekly instead of monthly. SOPs written down so consistency didn't depend on who was on shift. Workload redistribution planned for peak demand rather than discovered during it. That work produced 30% above monthly lead quotas and an additional USD 10,296,916 in revenue.

Safety taught me the same lesson from a harder angle. As EHHS staff at Daelim Philippines I administered the Permit-to-Work system and ran daily inspections, where the cost of a missed signal isn't a missed quota.

I'm now completing a BS in Information Technology, for a practical reason: the tools I wanted didn't always exist, so I'm learning to build them — Node.js, Laravel, Python, and a database layer that holds up under real volume.
